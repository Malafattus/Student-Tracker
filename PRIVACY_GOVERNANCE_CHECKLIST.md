# Privacy Governance Checklist

Use this checklist before storing real student or family data in the system.

## Purpose and scope

- Define the app as a workflow tool, not the school's official record of truth.
- List exactly what categories of student, parent, and counsellor information may be stored.
- Confirm what must remain in the SIS/LMS or Ontario Student Record processes.

## Accountability

- Assign a school owner for the app.
- Assign a privacy contact.
- Assign an operational security contact.
- Define who approves new staff access.

## Collection and use

- Document why each category of information is collected.
- Remove fields that are not necessary for counselling or workflow.
- Confirm which reports or uploads contain especially sensitive information.

## Retention

- Set a retention schedule for uploads, reports, and audit data.
- Define when closed items should be deleted or archived externally.
- Define what happens when a student graduates, transfers, or withdraws.

## Access

- Confirm which roles can view students, parents, files, and reports.
- Review team-based access regularly.
- Review parent-child access links regularly.

## Incident readiness

- Define how a suspected privacy breach is reported internally.
- Define who disables accounts and preserves logs.
- Define how impacted families or staff would be notified if needed.
