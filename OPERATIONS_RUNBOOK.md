## Daily checks

- Review failed login and lockout activity.
- Review queued/failed outbound emails.
- Review unusual downloads or exports.

## Weekly checks

- Review new staff, parent, and student access.
- Review locked accounts and password reset flags.
- Review team access exceptions.
- Confirm scheduled reminder and email tasks are still running.

## Monthly checks

- Review inactive staff accounts.
- Review non-compliant accounts in Security Center.
- Review backup success and one restore test outcome.
- Review retention and deletion needs for older files.

## When a staff member leaves

1. Disable the user account immediately.
2. Remove extra student/team access.
3. Review recent downloads and exports.
4. Reassign any owned tasks or records if needed.

## When a privacy or security issue is suspected

1. Lock the relevant account if needed.
2. Preserve logs.
3. Review downloads, exports, and recent views.
4. Notify the designated school security/privacy owner.
5. Document what happened and what was changed.
